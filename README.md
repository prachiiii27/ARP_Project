For this project, our team has been allotted the Private Jet category. So, we have selected the Gulfstream G650. The G650 is a premier ultra-long-range business jet produced by Gulfstream Aerospace.
The selected mission route is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD), Ahmedabad to Heathrow Airport (LHR), London, covering a great-circle distance of approximately
6,900 km.

The primary objectives are: 
(1) establish aircraft and journey specifications via an RFP;

(2) conduct phase-by-phase mission analysis to compute thrust requirements, fuel burn, and weight evolution;

(3) perform Parametric Cycle Analysis (PCA) to determine optimal engine design parameters; and

(4) carry out Engine Performance Analysis (EPA) using PERF to verify that the design meets all mission requirements including the OEI climb gradient constraint.
